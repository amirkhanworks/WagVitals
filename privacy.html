Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This is a plain-language draft of how WagVitals India intends to handle information shared through this website.

This draft is not the final privacy notice. We will complete and publish it after confirming our service providers, data handling practices and legal requirements.

1. What the form collects

If you use the waitlist or contact form, it asks for your name and email address, your city, and what you are interested in. Phone number and message are optional.

2. Why we use it

We intend to use the information you submit to manage the waitlist, send relevant launch updates, respond to questions, and follow up on pricing, partnership or press enquiries.

3. How it is handled

The form will send your submission to the service configured for the waitlist endpoint. The final notice will identify the form provider, any other service providers who handle submissions, where information is stored, and how long it is kept. Those operational details are still to be confirmed.

4. Your choices and deletion requests

You can ask us to stop sending launch updates or request deletion of the information you submitted. Email amirwagvitals@gmail.com with the subject “Personal data deletion request”. Tell us the email address or phone number you used so we can locate the relevant submission. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive information unless we specifically explain why they are needed.

5. Indian data protection law

This notice is being prepared with reference to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Their provisions have phased commencement. The final notice must be reviewed against the provisions and rules in force when the website and waitlist launch.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email amirwagvitals@gmail.com.
