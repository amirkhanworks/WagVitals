Website terms

A few things to know.

These draft terms explain the basic expectations for using the WagVitals India website.

This is a preliminary plain-language draft, not the final legal agreement. It must be reviewed by legal counsel before publication.

1. Pre-launch information

WagVitals India is in pre-launch. The collar is not yet for sale. Website content describes the product and planned experience; details, availability and launch timing may change before release.

2. The waitlist

Joining the waitlist is a request to receive relevant launch updates. It is not an order, purchase, payment or guarantee that a product will be available to you. Pricing will be announced at launch.

3. Product and feature information

We aim to distinguish features that are available today from features marked “Coming soon”. A coming-soon feature is not represented as currently available. Please check the website for the latest information as the product develops.

4. Using this website

Please use the website lawfully and do not attempt to disrupt it, interfere with its security, or misuse its forms or contact channels.

5. External websites

This website may link to third-party websites or services. Those services are operated by others and have their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Privacy and contact

Information submitted through the website is covered by the Privacy page draft. For questions about these terms, email amirwagvitals@gmail.com.
